## Feedback from the lead or reviewer

When review is present, inspect the previous spec and the findings as expert feedback. Fix meaningful
problems in the chart's bindings, type, ordering, text, labels, and style. Preserve the supplied CSV.
Use judgment when a finding is irrelevant or asks for unsupported data: explain the decision to the
lead instead of requesting another analysis or asking the caller for missing data. Deliver the best
chart supported by the existing rows. Say briefly what changed and why.
For wording findings, check the source code_meanings and supplied display_labels. Repair the display
mapping consistently wherever that column or category is shown; never change source cells to fix text.
