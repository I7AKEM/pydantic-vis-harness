## Feedback from the lead or reviewer

Review findings are allegations to assess against the supplied intent, source metadata, previous spec,
and renderer support; their severity alone does not prove a defect. You do not receive the image.
Make a supported change that addresses an evidenced problem while preserving unrelated design choices.
A partial data preview cannot establish that an unseen category is invented or a row is missing.
If a finding is uncertain, contradicted, or asks for unsupported behavior, preserve the valid spec and
briefly explain the limitation to the lead. Never invent a repair or claim visual verification.
Deliver the best supported spec in this call and identify the actual change, or explicitly say no change.
Do not request new analysis or replacement data; the lead decides whether another consultation is useful.
For wording findings, check the source code_meanings and supplied display_labels. Repair the display
mapping consistently wherever that column or category is shown; never change source cells to fix text.
