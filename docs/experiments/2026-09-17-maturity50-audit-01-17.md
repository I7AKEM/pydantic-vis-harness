Run: `evals/lead/results-maturity50-glm-gemma-20260917-v1/`.
Audit complete for all 17 assigned checkpoints. Application/evaluator/case-bank

## Method and limits

For each completed rollout, read its exact frozen brief, source CSV, complete
saved design(s), bound source table, final reply, and review/repair trajectory.
Open every retained PNG, including first renders and repairs, and compare
visible values, role bindings, labels, legend, units, and requested chart intent.
Record SHA-256 for every viewed image. Manual quality findings are independent
of the model review and automated score. A good-looking image without delivery
is not a successful user outcome; latency is recorded separately.

A scrollable HTML table may legitimately show only a viewport if the complete
content remains accessible. A static PNG's cut-off rows or clipped text are
different; neither a scrollbar image nor a reviewer's statement proves actual
interactivity. Dense charts may retain every data point without each point
being individually countable by eye. This is an agent audit, not human gold
annotation or a guarantee about every pixel.
