## Evidence and interpretation rules

- Read each rollout's `score.json`, saved `case.json`, request rounds, final
  artifact, spec, resolved config, and final reply. A score is an executable
  contract result, not independent visual truth.
- View all retained PNGs, including replaced previews, and record each exact
  SHA-256. Compare supplied rows and role bindings against the image.
- Distinguish suitable selection, visible defects, source/semantic fidelity,
  final delivery, false reviewer alarms, and avoidable extra calls. Do not infer
  model performance from a task that timed out before that model was called.
- A real scrollable table viewport may legitimately crop the visible window if
  the complete table is accessible. A static PNG cannot scroll; neither a
  scrollbar-shaped image nor a source CSV proves every rendered glyph is visible.
- Findings here are an independent agent audit, not human-adjudicated gold and
  not a guarantee covering imperceptible or unexamined behavior. Uncertainty is
  recorded explicitly. No old reviewer pass or old image audit is reused to
  certify these new outputs.

### Verdict definitions and limits

- `chart_suitable` considers the actual prepared-data brief and field meanings,
  accepting multiple valid charts. All 16 saved choices are suitable for these
  broad briefs; for 0042 that is a **spec-only** judgment, not a rendered success.
- `source_roles_faithful` assesses the final/last saved encoding and the source
  roles. It does not certify every visible decimal or invent meaning for codes.
  Case 0048's initial count-as-percentage error was fixed; the final role verdict
  is pass. Original source values and rendered glyphs are checked separately.
- `first_render_visual` refers to the first render attempt. `no_image` is
  appropriate when that attempt failed (0042, 0044). Case 0038's decimal
  precision materiality is explicitly uncertain; it is not a fabricated defect.
- `task_correct` includes final requested graphic delivery and unsupported
  final claims, but does not silently replace the separate latency measure.
  Case 0048 remains uncertain because its otherwise correct delivered chart
  is accompanied by an unsupported inherited visual-quality caveat.
- `reviewer_false_rejection=true` is confirmed in 0040, 0046, 0048, and 0049.
  It does not mean every finding in those cases was false. 0048's first unit
  defect is real; 0040's initial small-layout readability allegation remains
  unresolved at the actual provider input size. Native PNG legibility is not
  proof of reviewer-resized or browser-viewport legibility.
- `unnecessary_repair=true` in 0048 counts the attempted extra redesign blocked
  by the framework, not its justified first repair. 0049's executed Arabic
  localization repair is useful even though the review's percentage complaint
  was false. 0040 and 0038 repair necessity remain uncertain.
- All retained table PNGs here show their full rows. Nothing was rejected just
  for a legitimate scrollable viewport. No interactive scrolling or UI was
  tested, and a static PNG is not presumed scrollable.

## Maturity findings and next work

1. **Reliable final delivery is separate from publication.** Six cases have a
   persisted publication but omit the final image link; one of those (0041)
   never completes a final reply. Any next implementation should preserve
   explicit publication evidence and make final artifact references reliable,
   while measuring final-response latency separately.
2. **Keep the reviewer grounded in its actual evidence.** In 0040 a 30-row
   reference preview was treated as a full 85-row source, producing a false
   absence claim. In 0046 structured `revise/error` contradicts its own
   all-values-match finding. In 0048 a unique region label was falsely called
   duplicated; in 0049 an already-percent value was incorrectly ordered to
   rescale. These are representative candidate regression cases, not a reason
   to discard useful first-render inspection or to impose aesthetics in code.
3. **Preserve unit-aware numeric rendering and executable layout constraints.**
   0048 demonstrates an actual first-render percent format leaking onto a count
   axis. 0042 and 0044 choose 300 px indicator height that the renderer rejects
   because at least 362 px is needed. Earlier technical feedback can avoid an
   external render failure without becoming an automatic aesthetic workflow.
4. **Distinguish metadata-visible corrections from image judgments.** In 0049
   the saved English design contradicts explicit Arabic labels, yet a
   pre-review localization correction is blocked. This is inspectable from
   the design contract; it need not depend on an unrelated false visual error.
   The lead should still control whether and how to repair.
5. **Separate source preservation from exact displayed precision.** 0038 and
   0041 retain their full floating-point ratios in source/config but tables
   show 0.77 and 34.36. Ordinary display rounding is not automatically a data
   mutation. A promise of verbatim/no-rounding display needs a precise brief
   contract and a supported formatting path; adding a format option that the
   table ignores is not a verified repair.
6. **Numeric-claim scoring needs contextual diagnostics.** 0047 truthfully
   states that months 6–8 are absent; 0049 explicitly rejects a wrong 14.00%
   conversion. A numeric-token failure alone is not proof of an invented
   affirmative claim. Keep raw metrics, record these counterexamples in a
   separate diagnostic set, and do not globally exempt numbers or weaken
   required evidence checks.
7. **Latency is not synonymous with looping.** 0043 delivers a correct chart
   in one design/render/review but takes 92.31 s. 0041 publishes and then times
   out awaiting final completion. 0042 includes a 45.00 s designer timeout.
   Attribute time with observed lead/designer/provider spans before changing
   models or adding retry calls; cancelled attempts must be counted separately
   from completed framework usage.
